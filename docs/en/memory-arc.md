# Memory endgame arc

Execution contract for the memory endgame, condensed from the implementation plan; landed history stays in git: the stage order, each stage's normative rulings and gates, the silent-breakage register, the STOP conditions, and the measured cost baselines later rounds compare against.

Author ruling 2026-11-12: adopt ds-rt (headered blocks, per-fiber heaps, arena temporaries) and rework multi-word values to inline spans; effort L, multi-round, sequenced before surface rounds resume. Boxing stays rejected: per-field indirection the JIT cannot remove, no unboxing entry in the record's optimization queue, and BL-2's unboxed ADT story is the terminus.

## Stage order

- Stage one (landed) headered blocks behind the unchanged `Ref` id; stage two (landed) per-fiber heaps restoring E3 confinement while fiber-boundary words stay one word wide; the span rework (landed, multi-round) interleaving with stage two's later slices because the touched crates are disjoint and the shared seam is fenced by `guard_staged_count`, closed before stage three; stage three (landed last) arena temporaries over the placement pass, its escape predicate over the final span claim shape.
- Current state: every stage and every span-rework slice landed, so the live obligations are the residual slices recorded per stage, the gates, and the baselines below.

## Stage two — per-fiber heaps (landed)

- Store placement: each `Fiber` pool slot owns one `Objects` store beside `segment` and `callinfo`, swapped by `Vm::switch_in`/`Vm::host_release`/`run` (three ownership swaps, no word copied), a reused slot keeping its store so a foreign-held object outlives its dead owner's recycling; `net: Connections` lives on the sink side (`OutputSink`), and the id table, free list, intern map, and ds-rt `Heap` stay store state while counts live in block headers.
- Ref word, AUTHOR-RULED 2026-11-12 (option A): payload `(fiber u32, id u32)` at zero frame-word growth (`Value` stays 16 B, no dsmod impact), the mask once inside each of the three decode funnels (`read_ref`/`read_counted`/`ref_arg`) plus one line at each direct-match site, `RcHeader` keeping `flags` reserved for the dormant collector.
- Claim ledger and release-side exact ownership, AUTHOR-RULED 2026-11-12: a per-fiber `ClaimTable` per pool slot (crates/ds-vm/src/claims.rs) holds the counts a fiber's frames own; a foreign word's count releases only through the dec'ing fiber's own claim row (every honest crossing acquires one), a never-claimed cross-heap dec releases nothing while still refusing a freed id, the death walk decs through `dec_walk`, and same-heap decs keep the gift-count discipline.
- Executor principle (C4 amendment), AUTHOR-RULED 2026-11-12: every count mutation runs through the owning heap's own count funnels, a foreign heap only posting requests drained to quiescence, and the 48 split-convention native sites take the store-side router (crates/ds-vm/src/object.rs).
- Claim bands and alias rule: every claim carries an establishment band, recovered from the drop shape's trailing clear op and outranking the ledger, so Ref-free spans fire exactly once, dead claims on borrow copies refuse, and moved claims stay silent; the firing door clears the band in the frame word itself so the recycle door never re-fires. A word a borrowed registration names (borrow parameter, handler member alias, pattern field) never anchors a claim, so rebinding it re-establishes nothing, while owning hand-offs anchor.
- Drain doors and drive rule: the walk decs each taken claim against the word's own heap, settles cascades, and ignores bogus-word errors, running at the cancel-cut preemption point, the interrupt marking, the pool-slot reuse safety net, and `run`'s root reset, which closes the halted/trapped-root leak. A teardown hook must return to its driver: one that parks, dies (`HALT`), traps, observes a raised cancel flag at a backward branch, or calls a nursery control seam (open, spawn, join, boundary, cancel, all refusing under the teardown-drive flag) refuses with `Trap::ReentrancyRefused`, and teardown never surfaces a user-hook failure.
- E5 hook-run: each door fires the kept-frame deinit hooks through the hosted live drive (its own context when live, a takeover of its parked context otherwise) before releasing the counts; the claim directory is derived, not serialized, a scan per (version, function) recovering each claim from the drop's own staging before a `CALL` of the module's `$deinit` function, one hook per base slot and per consumed `Ref` word, innermost-first.
- Counts-stay transfer rule, AUTHOR-RULED 2026-11-12: a cross-heap dec is admitted through exactly two sites, the teardown drain walk (also the E5 deinit pass, closing the segment-truncate silent leak) and the joiner's single dec of a delivered word, the drain walk the only channel for foreign-holder accounting; host deliveries release while fiber deliveries transfer, the claim landing on the resumer or joiner at `die_current` and the nursery marking, the spawn seam acquiring one count per `Ref` capture for the child's entry frame. Worker amendment (2026-11-15, E3's sendability ruling): a fork pool worker receives the whole fiber — store, arena, ledger — by ownership transfer and returns it before any count crosses, so every count mutation still runs on the owning heap's own thread; a pooled child's scalar-only entry span is what keeps the in-flight foreign-dec class empty on the worker.
- Word crossings: a host face staging words borrowed keeps the host's claims (`spawn_fiber*`, `run`, `alloc_closure`, `intern_str`), while an in-language spawn acquires one count per `Ref` capture for the child's entry frame.
- Intern tables, AUTHOR-RULED 2026-11-12: each heap interns its own KSTR copy with counts local, the VM-global `kstr_cache` gaining a fiber axis, rows reclaimed at the version-retiring swap and the pool-slot reuse; a VM-global read-only pin-counted pool stays rejected.
- Pinned invariant: every body-produced word dies in-body (the join drops nothing) and every count-bearing word's classification stays visible to its uses and its binder; the closures kernel peaks at 6 live objects and exits holding 1, the root frame's tail-native abandon by design.
- Residual slice: the `resume_fiber`-interrupted detached child's claims are kept for the VM's life, its slot staying non-Dead so the recycle door only ever sees walked or exotic deaths.
- Gates: every existing ds-vm fiber/nursery suite plus effects_nursery.ds and nursery_surface.ds green unchanged; each child-death path (completed, cancel-cut, trapped-interrupted) drains its heap to zero; the spawn copy-in balances under the `diag` rc tripwires; KSTR loads serve across switches; no arena action at any switch; the teardown deinit fires exactly once per established claim under cancellation/interruption (band-gated, the `teardown_deinit` corpus and the band pins in `crates/ds-vm/tests/teardown_deinit.rs`); the diag-armed release build drives nursery_surface.ds and examples/nursery.ds to clean exits with zero rc tripwires, effects_nursery.ds refusing at its fetch-extern seam.

## Stage three — arena temporaries (landed)

- Temporary and deleted traffic: a produced count with no RCINC, staging forward, closure capture, exit transfer, or loop carry between birth and death, in the placement pass (crates/ds-lower/src/place.rs). Deleted traffic: the KSTR load's intern acquire, STRCAT fresh objects for dying temporaries, and the terminal RCDECs beside them, deinit hooks staying. Frames wire to the crates/ds-rt/src/arena.rs contract, and an arena-resident payload is a third `Value` domain beside counted blocks, invisible to dsmod, with the diag tripwires holding over counted blocks and the escape proof compile-time.
- KSTR seam: `produced_kstr` (on top of `produced`, disqualifying every claimed/copied/staged/loop-carried slot) marks a string-literal load with no count transit between birth and death, and `finalize` rewrites those to `KSTR_BORROW` (uop 0x03, no acquire and no claim row, the pool-kind check shared at module load), deleting their paired terminal RCDECs and rebasing labels by removed positions; a slot that ever transits keeps the acquiring spelling. The VM serves a borrow without an inc, `intern_borrow` pinning the canonical copy, the pins releasing at the fiber death doors and the heap-drain doors.
- STRCAT seam: `produced_strcat` (the same disqualification plus a `loop_exit` drop for words still owned at the loop's depth) marks a produced STRCAT result, which `finalize` rewrites to `STRCAT_ARENA` (Ref major 0xD minor 4) and deletes the paired terminal RCDECs. The VM serves it from the running fiber's arena, the bytes bump-allocating behind the frame's entry mark (pushed by a frame's first serving, popped at every RET and CALLT transfer, the death and drain doors `release_all`); `read_ref`/`read_counted`/native `ref_arg` refuse the word with `ArenaHandoff`, EQSTR/NEQSTR compare across domains, and a frame that cannot host the mark words (the u8 slot ceiling) keeps the counted spelling.
- Loop-back-edge reclamation, author-ruled 2026-11-12 with the seam: a loop whose body holds a designated serving gains `ARENA_MARK` (Ctl 0x9 minor 3) at the head and `ARENA_RESTORE` (0x9 minor 4) at the back edge, one I64 cursor word per marked loop with the head label on the inserted mark; a word crossing a back edge alive keeps its counted spelling by the `loop_exit` prune, only frames with designated loop temps paying, and labels rebase against both movements.
- Packed handle, author-ruled 2026-11-12 with the seam: `chunk:16 | offset:24 | len:24` packed in the u64 payload of `Value::Arena(ArenaStr)`, keeping Value at 16 bytes; every field limit is a named refusal (`ArenaExhausted { field }` with `Length` at the 24-bit length boundary trapped before the allocation, a hand-packed restore cursor naming its misuse through `ArenaHandle { class }`), never a truncation.
- Terminal-RCDEC placement audit, closed 2026-11-12 (the stage-three residual slice, the copy-out pair closed with it): eleven kernels' placed op streams (127 RCDEC sites classified) leave no remaining dec an arena serving could delete, so the designated-temporary classes are exhausted and the honest residual is the count-mandatory population (native-answer copy-outs, callee-object answers, checked-read hand-outs, loop-carried rollovers, capture copy-ins); any deletion must remove the callee's answer construction and the caller's terminal dec together, guarded by the factory_closure_balance pin, so re-open only with an author ruling and a hot population.
- Dispatch layout, landed 2026-11-12: the hot dispatch keys on the raw opcode byte, each arm owning its field bindings and canonicity refusals inline and every other byte falling to one slow arm through the decoders for the exact cause, so the per-instruction cross-crate `Uop::decode` call leaves the hot loop; the scalar readers are specialized matches on the frame word (`read_i64`/`read_f64`/`read_bool`/`read_tag`/`read_ref` behind one inline locator). Adding a uop adds one byte constant and one arm through a 226-entry table, so no arm re-lowers its neighbors; threaded side-table dispatch stays rejected, the scalar_loop bench (crates/ds-pins/benches/scalar_loop.rs) the pin against it.
- Gates: strcat_arena (across-domain servings and drains; the counted funnel's refusal; the 24-bit length trap before the allocation; the hand-packed cursor's misuse class; the back-edge restore's one-iteration footprint where a restore-less spelling grows past 4 MB at 20k iterations), the placement pins (a marked loop taking one mark/restore pair and no terminal dec while the native-staged link keeps its count; a back-edge-carried word keeping the counted spelling), the zero-RcOps and escape pins (transit words keep their counts), kstr_borrow (claimant churn, first-borrow, release semantics, borrow-load pool-kind refusal), native_frame_balance and value_match_e2e, corpus byte-identity against the base, and the string_temp gungraun pin: 8,205,025,167 Ir borrowed against 29,205,020,550 Ir acquired on one 5M-iteration kernel (~4,200 Ir per iteration, 3.6x).

## The span rework — inline multi-word spans (landed)

- Union regions and width flips: `union_shape` (crates/ds-lower/src/shape.rs) claims each field the lowest free contiguous region span, contiguity mandatory within one field, per-word kind stability, no word shared between one member's fields, and dead words zero-initialized; struct-typed fields open through `field_span` (the widened `field_slot`) with every member-field consumer moving whole field spans, the pre-rework union frame layouts staying bit-identical (union_layout_pins) and the u8 width ceiling holding. Union, tuple, application, and deinit-carrying field types still refuse.
- Claim accounting: a `Hook` carries the span's `Ref` word offsets, the claim folds its span's plain count words (nested claims keeping their own), `emit_drop` and `fire_claim` dec every `Ref` word of the span, `fire_claim` takes the escaping words, and `copied`/`staged_local` treat folded words as borrows outside the claim's own moved span.
- Native channel: `slide_plan` (crates/ds-abi/src/tailcall.rs) moves flat word ranges pairwise, so a multi-word parameter is consecutive slots and the width ceiling stays the u8 staging/nargs count and RETSPAN's u8 n; `NativeResult::Span` answers a discriminant plus a multi-word payload on overlay words `0..=n`, `overlay_native` bounds-checking the dynamic width at the write, and the CALLT/CALLTG tail paths riding a span to the original caller's overlay or trapping `RetSpanTooWide`; `NativeResult::Total` answers N words with no discriminant on slots `0..n`. `list_push` is the one variadic native, the list object carries a per-list element stride (0 iff empty, the first append fixing it and a disagreeing one trapping `ElementStride`), and the checked reads (`list_get`, `head`/`first`) hand the element's span out with one count per `Ref` word.
- Element spans: for-in and membership bind whole element spans per step, the gather producing one count per `Ref` word and the membership's structural compare decing the gathered counts before the chain; the copy closes carry element spans at the source's stride. A tuple member of a union region claims one word per element (`payload_fields` admitting the tuple atom, each element refusing wider than a word at `atom_shape`), every gather over a member's ordered fields walking positionally so the six re-span/widen/return sites plus `respan_into`'s index match stay monotone.
- Hosted seam and generic staging: `Vm::call_hosted`'s argument and result channels carry whole word spans, the result channel an inline-words answer with the heap buffer only past four words; `Staging` in ds-abi names the staged-span discipline (fixed, one leading span, one interior span), so a fold's accumulator stages between the leading and trailing named words, which `reduce` uses for its whole-span fold. `map`, `filter`, and `partition` take wider elements end to end, and `par_map` assembles each answer's words as one result-list element, a ragged answering width trapping `ElementStride`.
- Completion and fork closure staging: `ChildOutcome::Completed` rides the `FrameAnswer` the hosted seam's `DriveOutcome::Frame` already used (four words inline, the heap buffer past four), fed at the three entry-frame completion sites (a root RETSPAN, and a CALLT/CALLTG native tail's span or total), while a live resumer still receives the head word alone; the delivery acquires the receiving fiber's claim row, one per `Ref` word of the span, at the join's claim crossing (`Vm::fork_frame_end`). `Vm::spawn_closure_into` enters the child with the element's whole word span as the entry arguments and stages the capture block past the parameters at the CALLS copy-in coordinates, each `Ref` capture acquiring one count claimed for the child.
- Refusals that stay, each naming its own seam: union elements, deinit-carrying elements, a tuple arm in a member-test position, multi-word tuple elements, a callable element word keeping the scalar-provenance refusal, plus the five deinit refusals pinned in `crates/ds-lower/tests/deinit_e2e.rs` (closure-captured words, union payload fields, native/generic arguments, tail arguments, generic-struct deinit); reboxing stays rejected and no message names it.
- Gates: shape pins (member span disjointness within a union, exact zero-init coverage over whole claimed spans), the width-flip e2e (span_fields_e2e: flow, Ref-word balance, the refusal names), tuples_surface/lists_surface conformance deltas, the tutorial blocks (block 18 and block 19's scalar chain end to end, block 19's union-routing half and block 25's script waiting on their own seams), the fold's struct-element e2e (the combinators suite's interior-span, balance, and deinit-init pins), the tuple-element e2e (span_channel's read-back, Ref-word balance, iteration, and combinator pins, the member-test pin in span_fields, partition's tuple stride), and the placement plus diag suites green, the corpus byte-identical to the base under the diag-armed release binary.

## Silent-breakage register

- A half-done stage two frees an object early and the freed id reuses for another object (wrong values, no trap); a half-done span rework lets two members' payload words overlap (kind checks pass, foreign values read); a half-done stage three deletes the dec of a word that escapes (premature free). The gates above are the tripwires.

## STOP conditions

- Escalate to the author when any dsmod format change becomes unavoidable (none is identified, since the flat slot list carries spans), when the committed `RcHeader` layout needs a new field, when cross-fiber references force count mutation outside the owning heap, or when the KSTR ruling needs cross-heap counted constants.

## Measured cost baselines

- Stage two (release, medians of three interleaved A/B passes under the exclusive lock, against the stage-one base): containers +10.7%, closures +8.3%, combinators +7.6%, lists +6.1%; float-loop +2.6%, loops-arith +2.0%, maps +1.4%; fib 1-9% noise-to-small, no kernel crossing the 15% line.
- Adoption-scan deletion, against the stage-two base: containers -26 to -22% (about 412 to 319 ms), closures/combinators/lists flat, maps -2%. The copy natives take the fresh list's edge count on the one branchy walk their copy already pays (`inc_edge` per `Ref` word) and the producer natives release fresh element words' claims at adoption, so no payload is walked twice; remaining terms are closures' per-load intern-plus-acquire plus one ledger op and the per-call pending-queue settle.
- Map probe campaign, landed 2026-11-12 (against b9b7905): maps 993-1026 ms to 213-214 ms (-78%), containers and closures flat, the stage-one +52.9% recovered and the path now ~3x under the inline-entry base.
- Fiber path: a 100k spawn/join-cycle probe costs +20% wall, about 170 ns per cycle for the three store swaps, the spawn copy-in, the cross-heap pending-queue settle, and the slot-reuse claim walk.
- Gungraun pins: string_temp 8,205,025,167 Ir borrowed against 29,205,020,550 Ir acquired on one 5M-iteration kernel (~4,200 Ir deleted per iteration, 3.6x); strcat_temp 11.79G Ir serving against 24.45G Ir claiming, the seam's delta 12.66G; the back-edge restore holds one iteration's footprint where a restore-less spelling grows past 4 MB at 20k iterations; scalar_loop (crates/ds-pins/benches/scalar_loop.rs) is the absolute-Ir pin for dispatch litigation.
- Dispatch layout on the final binary: loops-arith 35.44G to 22.63G process Ir (-36%), wall medians over the full kernel set against the pre-STRCAT base improved on every kernel by 18 to 54%, past the ±2% acceptance gates.
