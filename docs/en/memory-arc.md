# Memory endgame arc

Execution contract for the memory endgame, condensed from the implementation plan; landed history stays in git.
Its sections are the stage order, each stage's boundaries and gates, the silent-breakage register, the STOP conditions, and the measured cost baselines that later rounds compare against.

Author ruling 2026-11-12: adopt ds-rt (headered blocks, per-fiber heaps, arena temporaries) and rework multi-word values to inline spans; effort L, multi-round, sequenced before surface rounds resume. Boxing stays rejected: per-field indirection the JIT cannot remove, no unboxing entry in the record's optimization queue, and BL-2's unboxed ADT story is the terminus.

## Stage order

- Stage one (the landed base) swaps slab entries for headered blocks behind the unchanged Ref id; stage two moves heaps per fiber, restoring E3 confinement while fiber-boundary words are still one word wide and the cross-heap surface stays small; the span rework is multi-round and may interleave with stage two's later slices because the touched crates are disjoint and the shared seam (fiber-boundary staging) is fenced by `guard_staged_count`; stage three lands last, its escape predicate defined over the final span claim shape.

## Stage two — per-fiber heaps over the fiber pool

- Boundary: one `Objects` store moves into each `Fiber` pool slot beside `segment` and `callinfo`, swapped by `Vm::switch_in`/`Vm::host_release`/`run` (three ownership swaps, no word or object copied), and a reused slot keeps its store so a foreign-held object outlives its dead owner's recycling; `net: Connections` lives on the sink side (`OutputSink`) so the native convention's two host references stay intact, and the id table, free list, intern map, and ds-rt `Heap` remain object-store state by construction while the counts live in block headers.
- Owner-encoded Ref word, AUTHOR-RULED 2026-11-12 (option A): the Ref payload is `(fiber u32, id u32)` at zero frame-word growth (`Value` stays 16 B, runtime words never serialize, no dsmod impact), the mask sits once inside each of the three decode funnels (`read_ref`/`read_counted`/`ref_arg`) plus one line at each direct-match site, and words self-describe so host-held and delivered words route anywhere; `RcHeader` keeps `flags` reserved for the dormant collector. Rejected: a VM-level id-to-heap registry (the same 48 native sites must widen anyway, plus 80 KB to 9.6 MB of registry at kernel scale) and an in-header owner word (RcHeader flags belong to the collector).
- Claim ledger: the teardown drain's count side is a per-fiber `ClaimTable` per pool slot (crates/ds-vm/src/claims.rs) holding the multiset of counts a fiber's frames own, and one table op rides each existing count funnel (RCINC/RCDEC, the KSTR acquire, the producer allocs and read-out retains inside the store router, the CALLS/call_hosted copy-ins claimed for the callee, the spawn copy-in claimed for the child) while the store-level edge face keeps edge incs out. Position bits were falsified first: they cannot know the placement pass's dup-vs-forward distinction (`f(s,s)` over a dying word and a live-source dup emit the same Mov/RCINC shape with opposite claim ownership, every rule set corrupts one of them, and an over-release with a foreign holder sharing the count frees a live object).
- Drain doors: the walk (take the ledger, dec each claim routed to the word's own heap, settle cascades, ignore bogus-word errors) runs at the cancel-cut preemption point, the interrupt marking (holding a cancel-payload claim back for its delivery), the pool-slot reuse safety net, and `run`'s root reset, which also closes the halted/trapped-root leak; a parked drive's context store is scratch, so the walk routes through the pool's live flag. AUTHOR-RULED 2026-11-12: the completed door probes ledger emptiness in O(1) and walks only when non-empty, making the drains-to-zero gate unconditional.
- Counts-stay transfer rule, AUTHOR-RULED 2026-11-12: a cross-heap dec is admitted through exactly two sites, the teardown drain walk (also the E5 deinit pass, which closes the segment-truncate silent leak: one walk, three doors) and the joiner's single dec of a delivered word; no other cross-heap count mutation may appear, and the drain walk stays the only channel for foreign-holder accounting. Evidence is the crossing census probed on the live tree: 4 bracketed delivery classes (spawn copy-in, dispatch capture copy-ins with `CLOSURE_NEW` incs, `call_hosted` argument/capture incs, native checked read-outs and producer retains) against 5 mutation-free crossings (child completion overlay, cancel boundary overlay, `join_nursery`'s outcome vector, `resume_fiber`'s return value and payload parameter, the host spawn faces); counts-move would add a mutation site at each of the five and still need an object move at the push-across-heap shape, since a parent-side count for a child-heap object is incoherent.
- Executor principle (C4 amendment), AUTHOR-RULED 2026-11-12: every count mutation executes only through the owning heap's own count funnels and a foreign heap only posts requests, so the two delivery sites and the queue-borne graph-edge accounting (cross-heap push/map acquires, the free cascades) are all owner-executed; cross-heap traffic rides per-store pending queues drained by the store-side router after each routed call (crates/ds-vm/src/object.rs), and the 48 split-convention native sites take that router.
- Deliveries: host deliveries release (the host never decs, matching one-store behavior) while fiber deliveries transfer, the claim landing on the resumer or the joiner at `die_current` and the nursery marking.
- Word crossings, verified sites: the in-language spawn seam acquires one count per `Ref` capture for the child's entry frame while the host faces (`spawn_fiber*`, `run`, `alloc_closure`, `intern_str`) stage words borrowed and keep the host's claims; `NurseryCancel` reads a general `Value` payload but the seam's staged slot is Tag-typed, so payloaded cancels and unions over generic applications wait on the span rework while hand-assembled `Ref` payloads already cross.
- Per-fiber intern tables, AUTHOR-RULED 2026-11-12: each heap interns its own KSTR copy, counts stay local, and the VM-global `kstr_cache` gains a fiber axis; measured loads are hot and mostly ephemeral (strings 125,028 at 20% cache hits, closures 800,004 at 0% since loop-body constant fragments die with their frames, lists 20,025, corpus programs 2-18) while distinct interned strings peak at 1-3, so duplicate cost at measured scale is at most 12 small string objects over 4 concurrent fibers. Rejected: a VM-global read-only pin-counted pool, whose traffic win (up to 800,000 incs per closures run) stage three deletes with the acquire itself and which would add a third never-counted word domain.
- C4/E3 restoration: rc.rs confines a block and its count to the thread that owns the `Heap`, the VM-global store was the one thing holding the whole VM to a single thread under that argument, and per-fiber heaps are what E3 fork-join ownership transfer needs.
- Pinned invariant: every body-produced word dies in-body (the join drops nothing) and the classification of every count-bearing word stays visible to its uses and its binder; the copy-out place names the physical word and binder registration tracks the place classification, which also fixed a factory-closure leak and a closure called twice reading a freed object. On the fixed base the closures kernel peaks at 6 live objects and exits holding 1, the root frame's tail-native abandon by design.
- Residual slices: the dead-pool KSTR row reclamation, the `resume_fiber`-interrupted detached child's claims (kept for the VM's life until its slot recycles or the VM drops), and the E5 hook-run side, since running user deinit hooks at teardown needs a live-drive form.
- Gates: every existing ds-vm fiber/nursery suite plus effects_nursery.ds and nursery_surface.ds green unchanged; each child-death path (completed, cancel-cut, trapped-interrupted) drains its heap to zero, the spawn copy-in balances under the `diag` rc tripwires, KSTR loads serve across switches, and no arena action happens at any switch (vacuously true until stage three wires frames).
- Diag verification: the diag-armed release build runs the fiber/nursery corpus (nursery_surface.ds, examples/nursery.ds) to clean exits with zero rc balance trips, and effects_nursery.ds refuses cleanly at its documented fetch-extern seam.

## Stage three — arena temporaries over the placement pass

- Contract: the ds-rt arena contract exists verbatim (crates/ds-rt/src/arena.rs: mark at entry, restore on every pop path through `RestoreOnDrop`, chunk-append stability, per-fiber ownership) and stage three wires frames to it.
- Temporary, in the placement pass's own terms (crates/ds-lower/src/place.rs): the per-frame ownership map already knows which words take an RCINC (`copied`'s dup), which forward counts at call staging, which move into closures, which transfer at exits, and which are loop-carried, so a produced count with none of those between birth and death is the temporary whose count traffic the pass deletes.
- Traffic deleted: the KSTR load's intern acquire, STRCAT fresh objects for dying temporaries, call-result copy-outs, and the terminal RCDEC; deinit hooks stay (emit_drop fires the hook, then the count goes). The call-result copy-out pair is symmetric for the loop-carried factory-closure shape since the stage-two classification fix (the copy-out place names the physical Ref word, so the loop body carries the dec; pinned in ds-lower factory_closure_balance), and stage three deletes the pair's two sides together.
- Word representation: an arena-resident payload needs a third `Value` domain beside counted blocks, a named decision invisible to dsmod since runtime words never serialize; the diag rc balance tripwires hold over counted blocks and arena words get hand-off kind validation, with the escape proof staying compile-time in the pass.
- Gates: linearity_modes_rc.ds and the deinit/free-order pins green (observable death order unchanged), the placement unit tests green; new pins: designated temporaries carry zero RcOps in lowered output, corpus arena-residency promoted to an automated check, and a gungraun instruction-count pin on a string-temporary kernel.

## The span rework — inline multi-word spans

- Union regions, landed 2026-11-12: `union_shape` (crates/ds-lower/src/shape.rs) claims each field the lowest free contiguous region span, with contiguity mandatory within one field, per-word kind stability, growth past the region end, no word shared between one member's fields, and dead words zero-initialized per word.
- Width flips, landed 2026-11-12 (the first slice): struct-typed fields open through `field_span` (the widened `field_slot`) — a field carries its whole flattened inline span, `MemberField` holds the word types, `words()` flattens, and every member-field consumer moves field spans (literals, widen/re-span writes, returns, patterns, per-word equality, zero-init covers each claimed word); the pre-rework corpus union frame layouts stay bit-identical (union_layout_pins) and the u8 width ceiling holds. Union, tuple, and application field types still refuse, and a deinit-carrying field type refuses (the inline span holds no claim of its own; per-span deinit claims own the seam).
- Claim accounting widened, landed 2026-11-12: a `Hook` carries the span's `Ref` word offsets, the claim folds its span's plain count words (nested claims keep their own), `emit_drop` and `fire_claim` dec every `Ref` word of the span, `fire_claim` takes the escaping words so a hand-off decs only what stays, `claim_base_of` resolves a folded word to its claim, and `copied`/`staged_local` treat folded words as borrows (an inc) outside the claim's own moved span.
- Refusal openings, landed 2026-11-12 with honest names: the field vocabulary beyond struct-typed fields, tuple elements (one plain frame word), a tuple member of a union, `guard_list_element` (one scalar word, the native channel named), `guard_hosted_lambda` (one-word hosted returns), and the `map_set` arm of `guard_staged_width` — each message names the native-channel slice as the unlock; reboxing stays rejected and no message names it.
- Slide: no redesign needed; `slide_plan` (crates/ds-abi/src/tailcall.rs) validates and moves flat word ranges pairwise so a multi-word parameter is consecutive slots, dsmod encodes FrameLayout's flat slot list verbatim so the format carries spans unchanged, and the width ceiling stays the u8 staging/nargs count and RETSPAN's u8 n.
- Native channel and heap lists, the next slice: `NativeResult` answers one word or tag+payload, so `list_get` read-outs and `partition`'s tuple result need a span channel while `list_push` staging already stages all element words under a variable staged count; the list stays one inline-payload block with multi-word elements as inline `Value` words at a per-list element stride taken from `list[t]`'s compile-time shape, retention per `Ref` word inside the span.
- Deinit re-triage: the five parked refusals (closure-captured words, union payload fields, native/generic arguments, tail arguments, generic-struct deinit) are each re-triaged under per-span claims with `fire_claim`; closes are recorded as they land and refusals that stay honest keep their names.
- Gates: shape pins (member span disjointness within a union, exact zero-init coverage over whole claimed spans), the width-flip e2e (span_fields_e2e: flow, Ref-word balance, the refusal names), tuples_surface/lists_surface conformance deltas, the tutorial's collections-of-records blocks 17/18/25 running end to end (still out of reach until the native channel), and the placement plus diag suites green — the first slice ran the corpus byte-identical to ca107a5 under the diag-armed release binary.

## Silent-breakage register

- A half-done stage two frees an object early and the freed id reuses for another object (wrong values, no trap); a half-done span rework lets two members' payload words overlap (kind checks pass, foreign values read); a half-done stage three deletes the dec of a word that escapes (premature free). The gates above are the tripwires.

## STOP conditions

- Escalate to the author when any dsmod format change becomes unavoidable (none is identified, since the flat slot list carries spans), when the committed `RcHeader` layout needs a new field, when cross-fiber references force count mutation outside the owning heap, or when the KSTR ruling needs cross-heap counted constants.

## Measured cost baselines

- Stage one (release, medians, exclusive lock): maps +52.9% (probe rows went from 40-byte inline entries with direct field reads to 16-byte descriptor rows whose candidate checks walk occupied byte, entry pointer, payload behind the header offset, kind checks, hash, a longer dependent-load chain per probe), lists +8.8% (pure locality loss at flat instruction count), combinators +5.5% borderline; fib and closures noise; strings, float-loop, and containers improved 6-10% (layout luck). The residual arc items, with no campaign started: bulk element-word paths want contiguous payload access.
- Cross-language frame at the same pass: tier-1 ds sits at near-parity with CPython 3.14 on integer loop dispatch, 2.4-5.6x behind on call/closure/list-object shapes, and 4-37x behind JIT-ed node; the call-heavy fib kernel is the worst python gap and call-path frame overhead is the clearest stage-two target.
- Stage two (release, medians of three interleaved A/B passes under the exclusive lock, against the stage-one base): containers +10.7%, closures +8.3%, combinators +7.6%, lists +6.1%; float-loop +2.6%, loops-arith +2.0%, and maps +1.4% are consistent small costs; fib spans 1-9% noise-to-small; strings is a few percent on a startup-dominated run. Maps +1.4% confirms the stage-one probe-row cost did not deepen, since the descriptor path was already paid on the stage-one side, and no kernel crossed the 15% line so the digs stayed one level.
- Stage-two mechanism, adoption-scan term deleted 2026-11-12: the scan — a re-walk of each claimed list's payload per alloc, releasing its `Ref`-element claims into the edge, formerly +30% instructions and the bulk of containers' +10.7% wall — no longer exists; the copy natives (cons/tail/collect) take the fresh list's edge count on the one branchy walk their copy already pays (`inc_edge` per `Ref` word, no ledger traffic, the vec moved into the adopting list), and the producer natives (`split`, `fs_list`) release fresh element words' claims at adoption, so a payload is walked once, never twice, while claim accounting stays exact (edge counts replace acquire-then-release pairs). Measured against the stage-two base (release, medians of three interleaved A/B passes under the exclusive lock): containers -26 to -22% (about 412 to 319 ms), below the stage-one base too since the fold also deleted cons's second element copy; closures, combinators, lists flat; maps -2%. Remaining mechanism terms: each of closures' ~800k KSTR loads pays the intern-plus-acquire plus one ledger op (the transitional term stage three deletes), and the per-call pending-queue settle; a `Ref`-free payload still pays the copy natives' one `Ref` check per word, deletable only by an object-model Ref-bearing bit (not queued; the kernel now sits under the stage-one base).
- Map probe campaign, landed 2026-11-12 (release, medians of three interleaved A/B passes under the exclusive lock, against b9b7905): maps 993-1026 ms to 213-214 ms (-78%), containers and closures flat. Each row carries a u32 FNV filter hash in a dense array beside the pairs and the probe resolves once per lookup into a text/identity/scalar class (the payload-local path), so a reject candidate costs one u32 compare with no id-table or payload load while a hash hit runs the exact check. Against the stage-one +52.9% (which puts the pre-stage-one maps base near 645-665 ms by the recorded ratios), the stage-one probe-path cost is fully recovered and net-positive, the path now ~3x under the inline-entry base; the residue is the O(n) association-vector scan itself plus probe-string materialization and interpreter overhead, no term above the 15% line, and bulk element-word contiguous payload access stays queued.
- Fiber path: the e2e nursery example is startup-dominated and indistinguishable between the sides, while a 100k spawn/join-cycle probe costs +20% wall, about 170 ns per cycle for the three store swaps, the spawn copy-in, the cross-heap pending-queue settle, and the slot-reuse claim walk.
