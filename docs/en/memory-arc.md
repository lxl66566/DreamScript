# Memory endgame arc

Execution contract for the memory endgame: the author rulings that still constrain the runtime, the gates that must stay green, the silent-breakage register, the STOP conditions, and the measured cost baselines later rounds compare against. All three stages and the span rework landed; their step-by-step transcripts live in git. Author ruling 2026-11-12: adopt ds-rt (headered blocks, per-fiber heaps, arena temporaries) and rework multi-word values to inline spans; boxing stays rejected (per-field indirection the JIT cannot remove, no unboxing entry in the record's optimization queue, and BL-2's unboxed ADT story is the terminus).

## Binding register
- Per-fiber heaps: each `Fiber` pool slot owns one `Objects` store beside `segment` and `callinfo`, swapped by `Vm::switch_in`/`Vm::host_release`/`run` (three ownership swaps, no word copied), a reused slot keeping its store so a foreign-held object outlives its dead owner's recycling; `net: Connections` lives on the sink side (`OutputSink`), and the id table, free list, intern map, and ds-rt `Heap` stay store state while counts live in block headers.
- Ref word, AUTHOR-RULED 2026-11-12: payload `(fiber u32, id u32)` at zero frame-word growth (`Value` stays 16 B, no dsmod impact), the mask once inside each of the three decode funnels (`read_ref`/`read_counted`/`ref_arg`) plus one line at each direct-match site, `RcHeader` keeping `flags` reserved for the dormant collector.
- Claim ledger and release-side ownership, AUTHOR-RULED 2026-11-12: a per-fiber `ClaimTable` per pool slot (crates/ds-vm/src/claims.rs) holds the counts a fiber's frames own; a foreign word's count releases only through the dec'ing fiber's own claim row (every honest crossing acquires one), a never-claimed cross-heap dec releases nothing while still refusing a freed id, the death walk decs through `dec_walk`, and same-heap decs keep the gift-count discipline.
- Executor principle (C4 amendment), AUTHOR-RULED 2026-11-12: every count mutation runs through the owning heap's own count funnels, a foreign heap only posting requests drained to quiescence, so the 48 split-convention native sites take the store-side router (crates/ds-vm/src/object.rs).
- Counts-stay transfer rule, AUTHOR-RULED 2026-11-12: a cross-heap dec is admitted through exactly two sites — the teardown drain walk (also the E5 deinit pass) and the joiner's single dec of a delivered word — the drain walk the only channel for foreign-holder accounting; host deliveries release while fiber deliveries transfer, the claim landing on the resumer or joiner at `die_current` and the nursery marking, the spawn seam acquiring one count per `Ref` capture for the child's entry frame; a host face staging words borrowed keeps the host's claims (`spawn_fiber*`, `run`, `alloc_closure`, `intern_str`). Worker amendment (2026-11-15, E3's sendability ruling): a fork pool worker receives the whole fiber — store, arena, ledger — by ownership transfer and returns it before any count crosses, a pooled child's scalar-only entry span keeping the in-flight foreign-dec class empty.
- Claim bands, alias rule, and the hook run: every claim carries an establishment band, recovered from the drop shape's trailing clear op and outranking the ledger, so Ref-free spans fire exactly once, dead claims on borrow copies refuse, and moved claims stay silent, the firing door clearing the band in the frame word itself and a borrowed registration (borrow parameter, handler member alias, pattern field) never anchoring a claim. Each door then fires the kept-frame deinit hooks through the hosted live drive (its own context when live, a takeover of its parked context otherwise) before releasing counts; the claim directory is derived, not serialized, a scan per (version, function) recovering each claim from the drop's own staging before a `CALL` of the module's `$deinit` function, one hook per base slot and per consumed `Ref` word, innermost-first.
- Teardown drive rule: the walk decs each taken claim against the word's own heap, settles cascades, and ignores bogus-word errors, running at the cancel-cut preemption point, the interrupt marking, the pool-slot reuse safety net, and `run`'s root reset, which closes the halted/trapped-root leak. A teardown hook must return to its driver: one that parks, dies (`HALT`), traps, observes a raised cancel flag at a backward branch, or calls a nursery control seam (all refusing under the teardown-drive flag) refuses with `Trap::ReentrancyRefused`, and teardown never surfaces a user-hook failure.
- Intern tables, AUTHOR-RULED 2026-11-12: each heap interns its own KSTR copy with counts local, the VM-global `kstr_cache` gaining a fiber axis, rows reclaimed at the version-retiring swap and the pool-slot reuse; a VM-global read-only pin-counted pool stays rejected.
- KSTR and STRCAT arena seams: `produced_kstr` marks a string-literal load with no count transit between birth and death, rewritten by `finalize` to `KSTR_BORROW` (uop 0x03, no acquire and no claim row, the pool-kind check shared at module load) with its paired terminal RCDECs deleted and labels rebased, a slot that ever transits keeping the acquiring spelling; `intern_borrow` pins the canonical copy, the pins releasing at the fiber death and heap-drain doors. `produced_strcat` (the same disqualification plus a `loop_exit` drop for words still owned at the loop's depth) marks a produced STRCAT result, rewritten to `STRCAT_ARENA` (Ref major 0xD minor 4) and served from the running fiber's arena, bump-allocated behind the frame's entry mark (pushed by a frame's first serving, popped at every RET and CALLT transfer, the death and drain doors `release_all`), `read_ref`/`read_counted`/native `ref_arg` refusing the word with `ArenaHandoff` while EQSTR/NEQSTR compare across domains, and a frame that cannot host the mark words (the u8 slot ceiling) keeping the counted spelling.
- Loop-back-edge reclamation, author-ruled 2026-11-12 with the seam: a loop whose body holds a designated serving gains `ARENA_MARK` (Ctl 0x9 minor 3) at the head and `ARENA_RESTORE` (0x9 minor 4) at the back edge, one I64 cursor word per marked loop; a word crossing a back edge alive keeps its counted spelling by the `loop_exit` prune, so only frames with designated loop temps pay, and labels rebase against both movements.
- Packed handle, author-ruled 2026-11-12 with the seam: `chunk:16 | offset:24 | len:24` packed in the u64 payload of `Value::Arena(ArenaStr)`, keeping Value at 16 bytes; every field limit is a named refusal (`ArenaExhausted { field }`, the 24-bit length boundary trapping before the allocation, a hand-packed restore cursor naming its misuse through `ArenaHandle { class }`), never a truncation.
- Dispatch layout: the hot dispatch keys on the raw opcode byte, each arm owning its field bindings and canonicity refusals inline and every other byte falling to one slow arm through the decoders, so the per-instruction cross-crate `Uop::decode` call leaves the hot loop; adding a uop adds one byte constant and one arm through a 226-entry table, so no arm re-lowers its neighbors. Threaded side-table dispatch stays rejected.
- Span shape and channel invariants that stay: `union_shape` claims each field the lowest free contiguous region span (contiguity mandatory within one field, per-word kind stability, no word shared between one member's fields, dead words zero-initialized); `slide_plan` moves flat word ranges pairwise, so a multi-word parameter is consecutive slots and the u8 staging/nargs and RETSPAN width stay the ceilings; `NativeResult::Span` rides overlay words `0..=n` (trapping `RetSpanTooWide` past it), `NativeResult::Total` answers N words with no discriminant, and `Staging` names the staged-span discipline (fixed, one leading span, one interior span) that folds and combinators stage through, the hosted result channel answering inline words with the heap buffer only past four.
- Arena domain and element spans: an arena-resident payload is a third `Value` domain beside counted blocks, invisible to dsmod, the escape proof compile-time over the crates/ds-rt/src/arena.rs contract; a temporary is a produced count with no RCINC, staging forward, closure capture, exit transfer, or loop carry between birth and death (the placement pass, crates/ds-lower/src/place.rs); a `Hook` carries its span's `Ref` word offsets and the claim folds its span's plain count words, nested claims keeping their own; collection elements ride whole word spans at a per-list element stride (a tuple element one word per element), where a disagreeing append and a ragged answering width trap `ElementStride` and the copy closes carry element spans at the source's stride.
- Refusals and audit verdict: reboxing (no message names it), union and deinit-carrying elements, a tuple arm in a member-test position, multi-word tuple elements, a callable element word keeping the scalar-provenance refusal, and the five deinit refusals pinned in `crates/ds-lower/tests/deinit_e2e.rs`. The terminal-RCDEC placement audit closed 2026-11-12, so the designated-temporary classes are exhausted, the honest residual is the count-mandatory population (native-answer copy-outs, callee-object answers, checked-read hand-outs, loop-carried rollovers, capture copy-ins), and any deletion must remove the callee's answer construction and the caller's terminal dec together: re-open only with an author ruling and a hot population.
- Pinned invariant and residual: every body-produced word dies in-body (the join drops nothing) and every count-bearing word's classification stays visible to its uses and its binder; the `resume_fiber`-interrupted detached child's claims are kept for the VM's life, its slot staying non-Dead so the recycle door only ever sees walked or exotic deaths.

## Gates that stay green
- Fibers and nurseries: every existing ds-vm fiber/nursery suite plus effects_nursery.ds and nursery_surface.ds green unchanged; each child-death path (completed, cancel-cut, trapped-interrupted) drains its heap to zero; the spawn copy-in balances under the `diag` rc tripwires; KSTR loads serve across switches; no arena action at any switch; the diag-armed release build drives nursery_surface.ds and examples/nursery.ds to clean exits with zero rc tripwires.
- Teardown deinit: exactly one fire per established claim under cancellation and interruption, band-gated (the `teardown_deinit` corpus and `crates/ds-vm/tests/teardown_deinit.rs`).
- Arena temporaries: strcat_arena (across-domain servings and drains, the counted funnel's refusal, the 24-bit length trap before the allocation, the hand-packed cursor's misuse class, the back-edge restore's one-iteration footprint); the placement pins (a marked loop taking one mark/restore pair and no terminal dec while the native-staged link keeps its count); the zero-RcOps and escape pins; kstr_borrow (claimant churn, first-borrow, release semantics, the borrow-load pool-kind refusal); native_frame_balance and value_match_e2e.
- Spans: the shape pins (member span disjointness within a union, exact zero-init coverage over whole claimed spans), the width-flip e2e (span_fields_e2e: flow, Ref-word balance, the refusal names), tuples_surface/lists_surface conformance deltas, the fold's struct-element e2e, the tuple-element e2e (span_channel's read-back, Ref-word balance, iteration, and combinator pins, the member-test pin in span_fields, partition's tuple stride), and the corpus byte-identical to the base under the diag-armed release binary.

## Silent-breakage register
- A half-done stage two frees an object early and the freed id reuses for another object (wrong values, no trap); a half-done span rework lets two members' payload words overlap (kind checks pass, foreign values read); a half-done stage three deletes the dec of a word that escapes (premature free). The gates above are the tripwires.

## STOP conditions
- Escalate to the author when any dsmod format change becomes unavoidable (none is identified, since the flat slot list carries spans), when the committed `RcHeader` layout needs a new field, when cross-fiber references force count mutation outside the owning heap, or when the KSTR ruling needs cross-heap counted constants.

## Measured cost baselines
- Stage two against the stage-one base (release, medians of three interleaved A/B passes under the exclusive lock): containers +10.7%, closures +8.3%, combinators +7.6%, lists +6.1%, float-loop +2.6%, loops-arith +2.0%, maps +1.4%, no kernel crossing the 15% line; the adoption-scan deletion against the stage-two base then took containers -26 to -22% (about 412 to 319 ms) with closures/combinators/lists flat and maps -2%.
- Map probe campaign against b9b7905: maps 993-1026 ms to 213-214 ms (-78%), containers and closures flat, the stage-one +52.9% recovered. Fiber path: a 100k spawn/join-cycle probe costs +20% wall, about 170 ns per cycle for the three store swaps, the spawn copy-in, the cross-heap pending-queue settle, and the slot-reuse claim walk.
- Gungraun pins: string_temp 8,205,025,167 Ir borrowed against 29,205,020,550 Ir acquired on one 5M-iteration kernel (~4,200 Ir deleted per iteration, 3.6x); strcat_temp 11.79G Ir serving against 24.45G Ir claiming, the seam's delta 12.66G; the back-edge restore holds one iteration's footprint where a restore-less spelling grows past 4 MB at 20k iterations.
- Dispatch layout on the final binary: loops-arith 35.44G to 22.63G process Ir (-36%), wall medians over the full kernel set improved on every kernel by 18 to 54%, past the ±2% acceptance gates; `scalar_loop` (crates/ds-pins/benches/scalar_loop.rs) is the absolute-Ir pin against threaded side-table dispatch.
