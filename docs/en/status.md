## The lowered subset

`ds run` compiles in-process (parse, static check, Perceus dup/drop placement, lowering) and executes on tier 1. What lowers today:

- Bindings and control: `var` locals with plain and compound assignment, `var` destructuring (one re-storable slot per element word, a `const` binder aliasing the source words), union-annotated `const` bindings re-spanning a bare member into the destination union, `while`/for-range/for-in loops with `break`/`continue`, `return` in any statement position, script entry folding an entry file's top-level statements into a synthesized `main` (a library parse rejects them, DS1014).
- Calls and values: monomorphic UFCS dot-calls; second-class parameter modes lower (`mut` a re-storable window over the borrowed staging — the caller's binding never observes the callee's re-seat — and `move` a consuming hand-off whose deinit claim rides the staging so the callee's own scope end fires the hook once, including the tail transfer and a trait member's dispatch); capturing and non-capturing lambdas, named fn values, callable-defaulted fn parameters (captures copy by value at closure construction), the trailing block argument staging exactly like a written trailing lambda argument over every callee class that takes one (named fn, UFCS dot, indirect call through a fn-typed binding); call-site argument widening into union-typed parameters through a staging temp; an omitted defaulted parameter staged at the call site in every spelling — free fn, inherent member, and trait dispatch alike, the trait declaration owning a member default's single staged literal; named arguments fill an enum case constructor's payload fields by name, while every function-call spelling (free fn, inherent member, trait dispatch, indirect call, combinator) refuses them at lowering — the checker accepts them, the uop set stages positionally; list and map literals and constructors with `list_push`/`map_*` (a `list[record]` or `list[tuple]` element stages its whole inline word span at a per-list element stride — a tuple one word per element, gathered positionally — the checked `list_get`/`head` read answering through the native result span channel, and the stride-aware closes — `cons`/`tail`/`collect`/`list_concat`, for-in, and `in` membership — binding whole element spans per step); `map`/`filter`/`reduce`/`partition` through CALLG with interpreter reentry, the hosted per-element call carrying whole word spans each way (struct-typed and tuple elements and answers, `reduce` folding at the fold's interior span-accumulator staging, `partition` splitting into the two lists of the native channel's tag-less multi-word total); `par_map` and `par_reduce` through CALLG — one spawned child per element, `par_map`'s answers joined in input order as one result-list element each at the callable's checker-proved span width, `par_reduce`'s fold chaining the running accumulator and the element, both parking the element's fiber at its own CALLG site through the interlock drive, both taking closure callables (the callable-defaulted `Ref` word is the caller's borrow, the fork staging the closure's capture block per spawn into the child's entry frame past the element's arguments, one acquired count per `Ref` capture claimed for the child); free generic fn calls — the ONE body lowers once with one Raw word per parameter over its own type variable, the entering site staging the instantiated evidence words after the true arguments in its outgoing-arg area (the checker's per-call record answering each declared bound under the site's substitution, a nested call from inside a generic body riding the calling frame's own evidence slot — the demand walk collecting the callee's own demands into the calling frame, a coinciding one through the shared slot and a lone one through its own — and a `mut` parameter over the type variable refusing at the body's planning with the overwrite contract); anonymous tuples with destructuring, including as union members (positional per-element claims in the union region) and list elements at the same flat-span vocabulary.
- Unions, errors, tests: `.ok`/`.err`/`?` extraction over the success-typed ambient with fallible native spans re-spanned into the call-site union, the failure bit pairing by the checked union's member shapes; `is` narrowing over written fn types and multi-atom `is` tests over written unions; `match` over unions (tag dispatch) and over non-union values (per-element constant test chain with guards and a join reconcile); structural `==`/`!=` over unions, a unit-tag operand comparing from either side, while one member's payload value against the union refuses at lowering naming the narrowing (the multi-atom use matrix is pinned in `multi_atom_uses_e2e`: whole-span bind/pass/return run, and every member-demanding use — arithmetic, narrowed arguments, interpolation, field reads, ordering, conditions, logical ops, calls through the value, list membership — refuses at its own checker seam); interpolation auto-`str`; char and byte literals.
- Ownership effects: declared `deinit` blocks run at the value's unique death (scope exit, early return, `?`, overwrite, frame exit) through the rc_bind hook, with the five honest refusals tracked in `docs/en/todo.md`.
- Dispatch: trait call sites through CALLG over impl member bodies; generic bodies lower once per impl with runtime evidence staged by the entering call site, a nested bound call transferring through the frame's evidence slot, a generic callee reached from inside the body (a free generic fn or a generic inherent member) collecting its own demands into the calling frame through the site's per-call record, a lambda inside the body carrying its demands in its capture block, and a spawned child carrying them in its entry argument area; a nested dispatch whose serving body is itself a generic impl's member stages transitively (the serving body plus its follow chain, two levels deep) and a nested runtime dispatch slides the chain one level per hop under the sentinel's exact arity (the target's parameters are the staged real arguments plus its own pairs' rider areas, three words per pair and at most one, so a rider-eaten gap traps; a substituted pair variable spanning more than one word refuses at the entering site — the one-word instantiation boundary), while a nested site in the runtime form copies the callee's area from its own frame slots; inherent dot-calls dispatch as plain local calls under `<target>#<member>`, a generic inherent impl's entering site staging its per-bound evidence through the ordinary CALL/CALLT. A dispatch tail (a trait site, a nested sentinel site, or the root frame's generic call) transfers through CALLTG — the CALLG resolution over the CALLT frame-reuse slide, keyed by the CALL-site inline cache with the full-lookup fallback, its evidence/instance words staged after the exit claims; a closure-staging tail whose callee word the scalar-provenance tracking proves count-free transfers through CALLT, the maybe-Ref words (a callable-defaulted parameter, a closure's Ref) keeping the in-place CALLS tail; free generic fns lower their ONE body once over the same evidence seam, minus the trait form's instance word. A trait's declared default body lowers once under `<trait>#<member>` as the dispatch target for a call whose solving impl spells no member: the body is the ONE generic shape over the trait's own `Self` variable (the receiver stages as the variable's single Raw word, the one-word boundary guarding a multi-word receiver at the entering site), its nested sibling calls on `self` ride the runtime-evidence machinery — the checker satisfies them through the trait-scoped `Self` assumption, so the entering dispatch site stages the serving member bodies (the spelled impl member directly, a generic impl member with its follow chain, an omitted sibling naming the default body again) plus the solved instance word.
- Natives: the pure `builtin` core in every VM plus the world surface (console, file, net) served by the platform handler the host installs behind the source's platform declaration — the CLI installs the default `std` on every run path, a package declaring its own platform names it in its world calls' FuncRefs and the host must install a matching platform or the install refuses (an unprovided capability is a structured install error); a total spelling over a fallible native prints `io|net error: <msg>` and halts.
- Runtime: tier 1 executes on `ds-rt`, whose block store carries every id and count (arena temporaries stay dormant until arc stage three, and the `diag` feature is the only balance-tripwire layer).

The boundary of this subset and every open seam are listed in `docs/en/todo.md`.
