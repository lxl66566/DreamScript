## The lowered subset

`ds run` compiles in-process (parse over the ambient's precompiled tree, static check, Perceus dup/drop placement, lowering) and executes on tier 1. What lowers today:

- Bindings and control: `var` locals and destructuring, plain and compound assignment, union-annotated `const` bindings re-spanning a bare member, `while`, for-range and for-in loops with `break`/`continue` (the for-in element count frozen at loop entry per the 2026-11-20 D3 ruling, the body's `mut` window over the iterated binding refusing at the check since 2026-11-21, DS3007, while the frozen count stays the runtime guarantee, an inclusive range ceilinged at `i64::MAX` exiting without the trailing step), `return` in any statement position (a value-consumed block refuses it, DS2039), and script-entry folding of an entry file's top-level statements into a synthesized `main` (a library parse rejects them, DS1014).
- Arithmetic: the int operators with their trapping families, the shift and bitwise trio [B7], the wrapping `+%`/`-%`/`*%` and unary `-%` [B7]; a float operand refuses at the check. Integer literals carry the i64 range at the check, the leading `-` folded in so `-9223372036854775808` writes i64::MIN.
- Calls and values: monomorphic UFCS dot-calls, receiverless inherent members through their type name or an instantiated application (`list[int].make()`), `mut` and `move` parameter modes, capturing and non-capturing lambdas, named fn values, callable-defaulted parameters, the trailing block argument on every callee class that takes one, omitted defaulted parameters, call-site widening into union-typed parameters, free generic fn calls (evidence staged by the entering site), and anonymous tuples. Named arguments fill an enum case payload only; a function-call spelling refuses them at lowering, and a written generic application validates its argument count and constructor kind at the check (DS2040).
- Collections and combinators: list literals, the list and map constructors, element-stride spans for `list[record]`/`list[tuple]`, the checked `list_get`/`first` result-span channel, the stride-aware `cons`/`tail`/`collect`/`list_concat`, for-in and `in`, `xs.len()`/`m.len()` through the free generic's List and Map instances, `m.keys()` through the ambient's inherent member (the `get`/`push`/`set` members keep their `list_*`/`map_*` native seams), `split`/`join` over the one `char` separator, `map`/`filter`/`reduce`/`partition` through CALLG with interpreter reentry, and `par_map`/`par_reduce` through CALLG over closure callables.
- Unions, errors, tests: `.ok`/`.err`/`?` over the success-typed ambient with fallible native spans re-spanned into the call-site union, `is` narrowing, `match` over unions and non-union values, structural `==`/`!=` over unions, interpolation auto-`str` dispatching through the solved `show` member, and char and byte literals (the byte escapes spelling 0-255, `\u{..}` up to 255); a multi-atom union's member-demanding uses refuse at their own checker seam.
- Derives: `show`, `ord`, `hash`, `clone`, and `default` on a struct, an enum, or a tag expand into ordinary impl items; `==` never derives [B9], and the ruled refusal classes stay (function-typed and `~Copyable` parts, non-data and generic declarations, the union-default ambiguity, a union inside the constructor brackets). The `default` family's application fields default through the collection spellings or the type's own `default` member.
- `from_json` and `to_json`: the receiverless `fn from_json(v: json) -> Self | parse_err` and `fn to_json(v: Self) -> json` synthesized per struct or enum target over the json face's typed readers, with `to_json: (json) -> string` the plain writer. The struct walk guards the object shape and reads one member per field (presence and absence, list fields through the array walk, a field spelling no reader through its own member), the enum walk matching the value's shape; the width fields ride the json face's readers (`byte`/`u32` as json numbers, `char` as a one-character string, `bytes` as a number array, 2026-11-19), an out-of-range or wrong-shape value answering `parse_err`. The refusal classes stay (a tag, a tuple field, two payloaded cases, a tuple payload, an empty enum, an optional nested inside a part, the 256-slot walk cap), ranked as item 5's.
- Ownership effects: declared `deinit` blocks run at the value's unique death through the rc_bind hook, with the refusal classes pinned in `crates/ds-lower/tests/deinit_e2e.rs`.
- Dispatch: trait call sites through CALLG over impl member bodies, generic bodies over runtime evidence staged by the entering site, inherent and receiverless members as plain local calls, dispatch tails through CALLTG, and a trait's declared default body as its dispatch target when the solving impl spells no member.
- Natives: the pure builtin core plus the world surface (console, file, net, time, and the tls capability, `http_get` routing https over rustls) served by the handler the host installs behind the source's declaration. The ambient's `bytes` collection (`bytes_of`/`byte_list` bridges, `len` through the `Bytes` instance, structural `==`/`!=` through the content compare, the checked `byte_at`), the width bridges (`byte_of`/`u32_of`, `int_of_byte`/`int_of_u32`, `u32.of(x)` trapping out of range while the ruled bare `u32(x)` waits on F9, item 75), the `list`/`map` structural equality riding the scalar `==` chain's one bit-structural float judge (a NaN reflexive, the two zeros distinct keys and unequal elements), the bytes `show`/`ord`/`hash` and `utf8_*` bridges, the `file_handle` and `net_handle` faces with their closing deinits and fallible reads, the ds-source json face, the total spelling halting on `io|net|utf8 error: <msg>`, and the die split (the builtin `trap` primitive answering a `Die` trap, the CLI's `$host.ds` member serving `die` for `ds run`/`dev`/`repl` alone, un-provisioned compiles keeping the name unknown).
- Runtime: tier 1 executes on `ds-rt`; produced string temporaries (KSTR borrows and STRCAT results) serve from the frame's bump arena under the placement pass's compile-time escape proof.
